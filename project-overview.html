Project Overview

MachOptDB — Database-Driven CNC Machining Analysis

MachOptDB

A relational database system for storing, managing and analyzing CNC machining experiments and supporting data-driven machining decisions.

Problem Statement

Different combinations of cutting speed, feed rate and depth of cut produce different machining results. A systematic method is required to store experiments, maintain workpiece and tool information, record results, evaluate energy and cost, and identify suitable machining conditions.

Aim

To develop a relational database system for storing and analyzing CNC machining experimental data and identifying suitable machining parameter combinations based on tool wear, surface roughness, material removal rate, energy consumption and machining cost.

Objectives

  • Design a relational database for CNC machining data.
  • Store workpiece and cutting-tool information.
  • Maintain CNC machine and operator information.
  • Record machining parameters and experimental results.
  • Analyze tool wear and surface roughness.
  • Compare material removal rate.
  • Analyze energy consumption and machining cost.
  • Use SQL queries for data-driven machining analysis.

Scope & Future Extension

The project focuses on database-based analysis of CNC machining experiments. It can support manufacturing engineers, CNC operators, production engineers, researchers and mechanical engineering laboratories. Future extensions include machine-learning models for tool-wear prediction, real-time sensor integration, predictive tool maintenance and automated parameter recommendation.
