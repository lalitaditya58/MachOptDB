Report Summary

MachOptDB demonstrates how a relational database can organize CNC machining experiments and support data-driven engineering decisions. The system connects workpiece, cutting tool, machine, operator, machining experiment, machining result, energy consumption and machining cost information.

The analysis shows that no single experiment necessarily optimizes every objective. E03 is particularly strong for machining quality, while E15 provides the strongest productivity, energy and cost combination in the sample dataset.
